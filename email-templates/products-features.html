Explore Physiomatics: Verified Capabilities & Architecture

Understanding how health technology operates before clinical adoption is essential. Below is an overview of the core capabilities currently operational across the Physiomatics web platform.

Computer Vision
1. In-Browser Video Movement Analysis
Direct webcam or smartphone camera pose estimation tracking 33 skeletal landmarks at up to 30 frames per second. No specialized sensor suits or external markers needed.
Launch In-Browser Tool →
Kinematics
2. Objective Joint-Angle Observations
Calculates geometric vectors across key anatomical articulation points including knee flexion, elbow extension, shoulder abduction, and spinal posture alignment.
View Biomechanical Vectors →
Form Validation
3. Exercise Guidance & Repetition Counting
State-machine algorithms detect movement phases (inflection, peak extension, return), counting valid repetitions and encouraging controlled tempo over rushing.
Explore Exercise Protocols →
Telemetry
4. Session Logs & Progress History
Maintains local session summaries so patients and clinicians can inspect compliance cadence, repetition volume, and range-of-motion trajectories over weeks.
View Progress Metrics →
Data Privacy
5. Strict Zero-Cloud Video Architecture
Crucial for medical confidentiality: All visual processing is performed locally inside the client browser. Patient video recordings are never streamed, stored, or processed on remote cloud infrastructure.
