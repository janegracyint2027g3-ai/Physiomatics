Enhancing Practice Visibility: Digital Movement Insights for {{organization}}

Managing patient recovery between clinical appointments is one of the most critical aspects of modern physiotherapy. Clinicians regularly prescribe home exercise programs, but assessing whether exercises are completed with proper form, frequency, and range-of-motion remains challenging.

Physiomatics was built to complement your clinical team’s existing assessment workflow. By leveraging lightweight, browser-based computer vision, the platform gives healthcare providers objective telemetry without introducing hardware overhead or complex patient onboarding.

Four Ways Physiomatics Complements Clinical Care:

📱
Zero Hardware or Installation Friction
Patients simply open a web link on their smartphone, tablet, or laptop. No wearable sensors to purchase, calibrate, or disinfect, and no app store barrier.
 
📊
Objective Angular Telemetry & Session History
Track estimated joint angles, repetition consistency, and pacing over time, giving clinicians concrete data points during follow-up reviews.
 
🛡️
Strict On-Device Privacy Architecture
Video feeds are processed entirely in the browser using on-device neural models. No video streams or patient camera feeds are sent to external servers.
 
🤝
Enhanced Patient Confidence & Engagement
Real-time feedback keeps recovering individuals motivated and clear on exercise execution between in-person appointments.
